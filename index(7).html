CORON CONNECT SELLER INVITATION

🎉 Be Our First Seller!

May negosyo po kayo sa Coron? Join Coron Connect and let more locals and visitors discover your business. Basic business listing is FREE during our launch.

Who can join?

  • Local products, souvenirs, crafts, and gifts
  • Restaurants, cafés, and food sellers
  • Hotels, resorts, hostels, and accommodations
  • Tour operators, guides, boat tours, and activities
  • Transport, rentals, and local services

What to prepare

  • Business or shop name
  • Category and location in Coron
  • Short description of your business
  • Contact number or social media page
  • 1–3 clear business photos
How to request your free listing:
Send your business information and photos to the Coron Connect page owner through a message. Your listing will be checked and added manually. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Free listing means: no fee to be included in the directory during the launch period. Sellers handle their own customer inquiries, orders, payments, and bookings directly.

Website notice: Coron Connect is a starter directory. Seller accounts, online checkout, payments, and automatic booking are not available yet.
