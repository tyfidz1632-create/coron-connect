Travel guide

Explore the beauty of Coron

Start with these well-known destinations. Always check local rules, weather, access conditions and fees before your trip.

Lake & viewpoint

Kayangan Lake

Clear water surrounded by dramatic limestone scenery. A signature stop on many Coron island-hopping trips.

Lagoons

Twin Lagoon

Two beautiful lagoons divided by limestone formations. Access between the lagoons can depend on the tide.

Freshwater lake

Barracuda Lake

Known for its limestone walls and unusual underwater temperature layers, popular with swimmers and divers.

Town viewpoint

Mount Tapyas

Climb to a panoramic view over Coron town and the bay. Bring water and take care on the stairs.

Nature & relaxation

Maquinit Hot Spring

A natural saltwater hot spring near mangroves — a relaxing stop after a day outdoors.

Snorkeling & diving

Siete Pecados & WWII Wrecks

Explore marine life and historic shipwreck sites with responsible, locally guided activities.
