GLOBAL SELLER INVITATION

🌍 Be Part of Coron Connect Global Mall!

Do you have a shop, product, creative business, or service? Sellers from the Philippines and around the world are welcome to request a basic directory listing. Free during our launch.

Who can join?

  • Product shops, handmade goods, crafts, and gifts
  • Digital creators and downloadable products
  • Restaurants, cafés, and food businesses
  • Hotels, stays, tours, and travel services
  • Businesses and service providers from any country

What to prepare

  • Business or shop name
  • Country and city/region
  • Category and short business description
  • Public contact or social media page
  • 1–3 clear product or business photos
  • Website or shop link, if available
How to request your free listing:
Send your business information and photos to the Coron Connect page owner through a message. Your listing will be checked and added manually. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Free listing means: no fee to be included in the directory during the launch period. Sellers handle their own customer inquiries, orders, payments, and bookings directly.

Website notice: Coron Connect Global Mall is currently a starter directory. Seller accounts, in-site checkout, international payments, shipping calculations, and automatic bookings are not available yet. Sellers and customers arrange transactions directly and should confirm shipping, payment, and delivery terms before purchasing.
